Meet Wes

Founder & Lead Psychotherapist

Professional, Compassionate Care

Finding the right therapist is the most important step in your mental health journey. Wes brings a unique, highly professional approach that avoids the "generic" feel of everyday therapy, focusing instead on a tailored, deeply engaged process.

With extensive experience in the medical and pharmaceutical intersections of mental health, Wes is equipped to help individuals, families, and groups navigate complex emotional landscapes.

"My goal is to demystify therapy. It shouldn't feel clinical or intimidating. It should feel like a safe harbor where you are heard, understood, and gently challenged to grow."

Whether you are dealing with a sudden life crisis, ongoing anxiety, or simply a desire to understand yourself better, Wes tailors his therapeutic approach to fit your specific needs, pulling from Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Psychodynamic approaches, and mindfulness techniques.

A Safe, Elevated Environment

Your healing environment matters. Our office is designed to feel more like a high-end, comfortable living space than a clinical medical facility.

From the moment you walk into the waiting room, you'll experience a calming atmosphere. Natural light, subtle therapeutic greens, and comfortable seating set the tone for a productive, peaceful session.
