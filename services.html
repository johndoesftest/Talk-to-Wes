Our Services

Comprehensive psychotherapy for children, adolescents, and adults. We provide a safe space designed to accommodate your schedule, comfort level, and specific therapeutic needs.

Individual Therapy

A safe, private environment to discuss your feelings, thoughts, and behaviors. Whether you are dealing with anxiety, depression, trauma, or life transitions, individual therapy provides tailored coping strategies.

Perfect for:

  • Managing severe stress and burnout
  • Processing grief and loss
  • Overcoming phobias or panic attacks
  • Personal growth and self-discovery

Family Therapy

Designed to help families navigate difficult times, improve communication, and resolve conflicts. We create a structured, neutral space where every family member can be heard without judgment.

Perfect for:

  • Parent-child conflicts and miscommunication
  • Blended family integration
  • Navigating divorce or separation
  • Supporting a family member with mental illness

Group Therapy

A powerful way to realize you are not alone. Group sessions involve connecting with peers facing similar issues under the guidance of a professional, fostering mutual support and shared growth.

Perfect for:

  • Social anxiety and interpersonal skills
  • Addiction recovery support
  • Teenagers facing peer pressure
  • Building a supportive community network

Flexible Session Formats

We understand that making time for mental health can be challenging. That's why we offer multiple formats to ensure you get the care you need, when you need it.

In-Person Sessions

Visit our modern, beautifully designed office space. Enjoy a calming, professional environment where you can step away from the distractions of daily life and focus entirely on your well-being.

Teletherapy (Online Sessions)

Access high-quality psychotherapy from the comfort of your home. We use secure, HIPAA-compliant video platforms to conduct sessions with the same level of care and professionalism as an office visit. Ideal for busy professionals or those with mobility restrictions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about beginning therapy with us.

Do you accept insurance?

We are currently out-of-network providers for most major insurance companies. However, we can provide you with a "Superbill" that you can submit to your insurance provider for potential partial reimbursement depending on your out-of-network benefits.

How long does a typical session last?

Individual and family therapy sessions typically last for 50 minutes. Group therapy sessions often run for 75 to 90 minutes to allow everyone adequate time to share.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require a minimum of 24 hours notice for any cancellations or rescheduling. Appointments canceled with less than 24 hours notice will be subject to a late cancellation fee.

Is everything I say completely confidential?

Yes. Confidentiality is the cornerstone of psychotherapy. What you discuss in session is legally protected and strictly confidential, with a few very specific legal exceptions regarding immediate harm to yourself or others, which will be discussed in your first session.

Ready to Take the First Step?

Send us a message and we will get back to you within 24 hours.

If you are in a life-threatening situation, please call 911 or the National Suicide Prevention Lifeline at 988 immediately. Help is available 24/7.
